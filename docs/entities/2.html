Muthoot Finance reports highest-ever standalone PAT of Rs 10,134 crore for FY26, up 95% YoY; consolidated gold loan AUM at Rs 1,65,000 crore Standalone gold loan AUM reached Rs 1,64,000 crore and PAT Rs 10,134 crore (+95% YoY). Standalone yield improved to ~20.76% from 20.34% last quarter, aided by ~Rs 85 crore one-offs (auction income Rs 50 crore + ARC Rs 35 crore) and a 0.5%-1% pricing increase. Stage 3 NPA rose to 2.34% (from 3.35% in Mar-25) on absolute basis due to shift to borrower-wise classification per RBI guidance; LTV on stage 3 assets including interest is ~58%. Subsidiary Muthoot Money upgraded to CRISIL AA+ Stable; its gold loan AUM grew 151% to Rs 9,794 crore with PAT of Rs 338 crore vs Rs 12 crore last year. Management guided 15% AUM growth for FY27 standalone, with 200-300 new branches planned.

Document rolled from Q3FY26 to Q4FY26/FY26 full-year results. Standalone PAT surged 95% YoY to ₹10,134 Cr (FY26) and 105% YoY to ₹3,086 Cr (Q4FY26), with total income up 61% YoY to ₹27,600 Cr for FY26. Consolidated AUM grew 49% YoY to ₹1,81,917 Cr, supported by strong gold loan growth (54% YoY); Belstar MFI AUM grew only 3% YoY, signalling stress in the microfinance subsidiary. New Labour Code one-off cost now quantified at ₹38 Cr (standalone) for FY26, smaller than initially flagged.
