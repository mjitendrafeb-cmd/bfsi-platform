Detailed Rationale

Crisil Ratings has upgraded its ratings on the bank facilities of Religare Broking Limited (RBL) to ‘Crisil BBB+/Stable/Crisil A2+’ from 'Crisil BBB/Stable/Crisil A3+’.

 

The upgrade reflects the company’s strengthened capital position and improved financial flexibility following the change in ownership of Religare Enterprises Limited (REL); which will support the growth plans of RBL. This is evidenced by the planned capital infusion of Rs 200 crore in fiscal 2027, Rs 100 crore of which has been infused in August 2026.  Additional capital would also be available to support growth as and when required.

 

RBL is a retail broking entity that offers a full range of services across equity, commodity and currency segments. It also offers other ancillary services such as distribution of bonds, mutual funds and other financial products and selling insurance policies as an Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (IRDAI)-registered corporate agent. RBL is a wholly owned subsidiary of REL.

 

The ratings factors in the company’s strengthened capital position, supported by enhanced financial flexibility, expected improvement in earnings profile over the medium term, and adequate risk management systems. These strengths are partially offset by the modest market share of the company in the equity broking segment and susceptibility to intense competition and uncertainties inherent to capital market business.

 

With the successful conclusion of the Open Offer in February 2025 and the subsequent reconstitution of the board of REL with representation from the Burman group (Promoters of REL) in July 2025, the Burman group has committed to support Religare group’s financial services businesses. In July 2025, REL had announced a preferential issue of warrants of Rs 1,500 crore of which Rs 750 crore is committed by the Burman group and Rs. 200 crores have been earmarked for RBL.

 

As on June 30, 2026, RBL reported networth of Rs 283 crore and gearing of 1.5 times. For the first quarter of fiscal 2027, RBL reported profit after tax (PAT) of Rs 4 crore translating to an annualised return on equity (RoE) of 5.7% against Rs 14 crore and 5.3%, respectively, in fiscal 2026.

 

RBL has a wholly owned subsidiary, Religare Digital Solutions Limited (RDSL), which was incorporated on April 7, 2022, to carry out the e-governance and financial inclusion business. RBL had filed a Scheme of Arrangement with the Hon’ble National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT) for transfer and vesting of its business to RDSL, on a going concern basis by way of a slump sale[1]. The Hon'ble NCLT approved the scheme in June 2025, and the transfer was effectuated in August 2025 with an appointed date of April 1, 2022. RBL has one more subsidiary, Religare Commodities Limited.

 

Crisil Ratings will continue to track developments in aspects such as the evolution of the business profile of REL and its other subsidiaries following the capital infusion into these subsidiaries, synergies built across the Religare group, the strategic importance of RBL within the Religare Group and regularity of need-based financial support to RBL.

[1] This transaction is in accordance with Section 2(42C) read with section 50B of the Income Tax Act, 1961

Analytical Approach

Crisil Ratings has analysed the standalone business, financial and managerial risk profiles of RBL.

Key Rating Drivers - Strengths

Strengthened capital position, supported by enhanced financial flexibility

RBL’s capital position has strengthened, supported by enhanced financial flexibility following the change in ownership of REL. The Burman group holds a controlling stake in REL, thereby demonstrating the promoters' long-term commitment towards supporting the growth, capitalisation and overall financial profile of the Religare group (REL and its subsidiaries).

 

As on June 30, 2026, RBL reported networth of Rs 283 crore as against Rs 279 crore as on March 31, 2026, and Rs 265 crore on March 31, 2025. Correspondingly, gearing stood at 1.5 times, 1.6 times and 0.7 time as on respective dates; it is expected to remain at 1–2 times on a steady-state basis.

 

Of the proceeds of the preferential issue of warrants, REL has earmarked a capital support of Rs 200 crore for RBL, of which Rs 100 crore has already been infused in August 2026, with the balance expected by March 2027. RBL will continue to receive funding support, given its strategic importance within the broader Religare group ecosystem and the criticality of the broking franchise to the group's financial services offerings.

 

The resulting capital buffer is expected to support growth while maintaining adequate cushion against business and market-related risks.

 

Expected improvement in earnings profile over the medium term

RBL’s earning profile is likely to benefit from operating leverage-led efficiencies, growth in active clients and increasing penetration of higher-yielding products such as distribution and margin funding. While profitability currently remains modest due to declining income and a relatively high operating expense base driven by investments in branch expansion, workforce augmentation and technology infrastructure, increasing scale of operations is expected to support better absorption of fixed costs. Consequently, the cost-to-income ratio should moderate over the medium term from the currently elevated levels of 93%, supporting improvement in profitability and RoE metrics.

 

RBL reported PAT of Rs 14 crore and return on networth of 5.3% in fiscal 2026; these metric were Rs 4 crore and 5.7% (annualised) for the first quarter of fiscal 2027. Improvement in earnings and cash accrual generation should further strengthen the company's financial risk profile while supporting growth in its business franchise over the medium term.

 

Adequate risk management systems

RBL has adequate risk management systems, which support the business risk profile of the company. The company endeavors to adhere to all the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) and exchange-prescribed regulations by instilling requisite systems and processes. The company sets client trading limits upfront and monitors client exposure on a real-time basis. It also sets scrip-wise exposure limits to keep a check on illiquid scrips or scrips under any kind of surveillance. The upfront margin along with peak margin is collected necessarily and clients are required to maintain an adequate margin as prescribed by exchanges. In case of adverse/volatile price movements, real-time risk-based square off can be initiated at any time during the day.

Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses

Modest market share in the broking segment

RBL, though a seasoned player in the securities industry, had a market share of 0.26% (0.31% for fiscal 2025) in the cash segment and 0.02% (0.02%) in the futures and options segment basis volumes for fiscal 2026. Overall market share was 0.02% for fiscals 2025 and 2026. The drop in market share is primarily on account of few corrective steps taken by the new management in fiscal 2026 of in terms of downward pricing alignments, which led to loss of revenue in fiscal 2026 compared to fiscal 2025. The higher pricing also led to loss of few customers in fiscal 2025, the impact of which was seen in the subsequent year. While the company has a long track of operations, its services remained focused on its existing client base.

 

The company’s gross margin trading facility book increased from Rs. 216 Crores as at March 31, 2026 to Rs 364 crore as at September 30, 2026 reflecting a growth of ~68%. The book is expected to scale further over the next few quarters.

 

Beyond this, the company continues to offer a variety of financial products and services within the retail broking and investment space. This is expected to support its future growth prospects.

 

Exposure to intense competition and risk of regulatory changes

The business of RBL is confined to the capital market industry, which is intensely competitive with multiple players offering low-cost products. The broking industry has seen a huge transformation in the past decade, with technology-based discount brokers entering and dominating the market. The management of RBL is undertaking several steps to generate scale-based growth and restore its market position in the retail brokerage space and other allied services. The company's broking business also remains exposed to economic, political and social factors that drive investor sentiments. The ability to maintain a resilient business model and grow market share while managing competition will be a key monitorable.

 

The capital markets industry has recently faced two significant regulatory changes, presenting a challenge to market participants. The introduction of a higher securities transaction tax on February 1, 2026, marks the second increase in two years, and is expected to impact futures volumes. Furthermore, the Reserve Bank of India’s (RBI’s) guidelines, issued on February 13, 2026, stipulate that bank guarantees for proprietary trading will require 100% collateral, effective July 1, 2026, up from the previous 50% requirement. This change may prompt proprietary traders to explore alternative funding options, such as equity or market borrowing, to sustain current volumes. However, RBL’s diversified business profile, supported by a meaningful retail franchise and the absence of proprietary trading activities, serves as a key mitigating factor and partially cushions the impact of these regulatory changes on its earnings profile.

 

Fundamentally, while these revised regulations will benefit the broking industry in the long term by increasing transparency and lowering risks for customers, the changes may reduce the trading opportunities for proprietary trades, along with increasing the compliance costs for brokers and require them to adapt their business models to keep pace. Thus, the group’s ability to realign its trading strategies to absorb any long-term regulatory impact on transaction volumes and higher tax and compliance costs will remain monitorable over the medium term.

Liquidity Adequate

RBL’s total cash and bank balances stood at Rs 794.06 crores as on September 30, 2026, of which Rs 772.09 crores was cash and bank balance and the remaining was funds held in the form of lien marked fixed deposits (FDs) that were pledged with banks (against bank guarantees and other credit facilities) and stock exchanges for margin purposes. The unutilised margin offers adequate liquidity cushion to RBL in case of any eventualities. The company also has an unutilised bank limit of Rs 137.69 crores (including overdraft against fixed deposit of Rs 52.69 crores) as on September 30 , 2026.

Outlook Stable

RBL will continue to maintain a comfortable capitalisation position. The ability to improve its market position and profitability will have to be monitored.

Rating Sensitivity factors

Upward factors

  • Significant and sustained improvement in market share
  • Cost-to-income ratio (operating expense/ total income) improving to, and remaining below, 60% on a steady-state basis, thereby improving the overall earnings profile

 

Downward factors

  • Deterioration in capitalisation metrics
  • Weakening of the earnings profile or cost-to-income ratio (operating expense/total income) increasing to and remaining above 85% for a prolonged period

About the Company

RBL, a wholly owned subsidiary of REL, provides a comprehensive range of services to retail clients. RBL is a member of all major stock exchanges in India and registered depository participant with both National Securities Depository Limited and Central Depository Services (India) Limited. The broking business was started by the Religare group more than 25 years ago and currently offers a full range of broking services such as equities, commodities, and currencies, depository participant services, distribution of bonds, mutual fund and other investment products etc., besides selling insurance policies as an IRDAI-registered corporate agent and offering research capabilities to customers. 

 

RBL is also registered with Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority and SEBI to act as Point of Presence for National Pension Scheme and Registrars to an issue and share respectively.

 

RBL, through its subsidiary—RDSL—also offers government services, financial inclusion services and citizen electronic services and other ancillary and allied services including but not limited to the following: permanent account number, tax deduction and collection account number and electronic tax deducted at source returns, business correspondent for banking services, Bharat bill payment systems and recharges, ticketing for airline, railways, bus or tourism through any online/offline platform, digital signature certificate and token and central record keeping agency facilitation centre.

 

The company operates through a network of 51 branches, 1,100+ broking business partners and 65,000+ e-governance franchisees in 400+ cities across India. It has more than 1 million broking clients.

Any other information:

RBL is a wholly owned subsidiary of REL and the retail broking arm of the Religare group.

 

Since inception, REL has infused equity capital of Rs 330.80 crore in RBL to support the latter’s growth and does not intend to start upstreaming dividends any time soon.

 

In March 2023, Religare Finvest Limited (RFL), a subsidiary of REL, engaged in the business of SME Finance had completed a One Time Settlement (OTS) with 16 lenders. Overall, the entity had repaid ~Rs 2,200 crore to the lenders as part of OTS and closed all the legacy issues borne out of erstwhile promoters’ actions. Thus, there are no expected foreseeable implications of legacy issues faced by this entity on any of the other group companies going ahead. On July 22, 2025, the Hon’ble High Court of Delhi quashed the classification of  RFL’s account as fraudulent by its lenders and directed that RFL’s name be removed from the Central Fraud Registry. As on date, the group has stated that there are no willful defaulter proceedings pending against RFL. Further, on July 23, 2025, RBI removed the corrective action plan imposed on RFL.

 

Crisil Ratings has taken note of the change in ownership of REL, and the consequent reconstitution of the board of REL. On February 19 and 20, 2025, the Burman Group—through its acquiring entities M B Finmart Private Limited, Puran Associates Private Limited, VIC Enterprises Private Limited and Milky Investment & Trading Company [collectively referred to as the Burman group acquiring companies]—announced the conclusion of its open offer for sale made to the public shareholders of REL in terms of the SEBI (Substantial Acquisitions of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011. Following this, the Burman group acquiring companies were classified as promoters of REL and the Promoter and Promoter Group holds a 31.26% stake in REL as on September 30, 2026.
