Detailed Rationale

Crisil Ratings has assigned its ‘Crisil AAA/Stable’ rating to the Rs 2000 crore subordinated debt of Tata Capital Limited (TCL) and has reaffirmed its ‘Crisil AAA/Crisil PPMLD AAA/Crisil AA+/Stable/Crisil A1+’ ratings on the existing debt instruments.

 

Crisil Ratings has withdrawn its ratings on non convertible debentures of Rs. 1075 crore, and subordinated debt of Rs 200 crore (See Annexure 'Details of rating withdrawn' for details) at the company’s request and on redemption. This is in line with the Crisil Ratings’ ratings withdrawal policy. Crisil Ratings has received independent confirmation that these instruments are fully redeemed.

 

TCL along with its and its subsidiaries TCHFL, Tata Securities Ltd and Tata Capital Pte Ltd, Singapore, together referred herein as the TCL group.  

 

The rating continues to factor in the TCL group’s majority ownership by, and strategic importance of the financial services business to, the parent, Tata Sons Pvt Ltd (Tata Sons; rated ‘Crisil AAA/Stable’). This is in line with the group's focus on domestic consumption as a key theme in their growth philosophy.

 

TCL is the third largest non-banking financial company (NBFC) in India, with consolidated AUM of Rs 3,08,030 crore as on September 30, 2026 from Rs 2,77,275 crore as on March 31, 2026. The loan book of TCL is fairly diversified across various products within retail as well as small and medium enterprises (SME) and corporate segments. TCHFL’s loan portfolio stood at Rs 95,098 crore as on September 30, 2026 from Rs 86,653 crore as on March 31, 2026, which comprises of housing loans, loan against property (LAP) and Builder Loans.

 

The ratings also continue to factor in the comfortable capitalisation to support the medium-term growth plans of the group, diversified resource profile and comfortable asset quality metrics. These strengths are partially offset by moderate earnings profile of the group.

 

The rating on the perpetual bonds reflects the extent of buffer over the regulatory capital adequacy requirements, and high financial flexibility due to the ownership of Tata Sons. Crisil Ratings believes that it will continue to maintain this cushion going forward.

Analytical Approach

Crisil Ratings has combined the business and financial risk profiles of TCL and its subsidiaries TCHFL, Tata Securities Ltd and Tata Capital Pte Ltd, Singapore, together referred herein as the TCL group. This is because they have significant operational and management linkages and operate under the common brand. The ratings also factor in the strong support from the parent, Tata Sons, given the strategic importance of TCL and its subsidiaries as the principal vehicle of non-captive lending in the group and Tata Sons’ strong articulation of support. The rating on the perpetual bonds additionally takes into account restriction to TCL group from servicing these instruments if it breaches the minimum regulatory capital requirement, or if the regulator denies permission to the company to make payments of interest and principal, if it reports losses.

Key Rating Drivers - Strengths

Strategic importance to, and expectation of strong support from the ultimate parent, Tata Sons:

TCL group is expected to continue to receive strong support from the ultimate parent, Tata Sons. This is due to Tata Sons’ majority ownership in the TCL group, coupled with the increasing importance of the financial services business to the Tata group.

 

TCL is a majority-owned subsidiary of Tata Sons (78.8% stake in TCL’s equity shares on September 30, 2026) and is strategically important to the Tata Group (85.4% stake of TCL’s equity shares on September 30, 2026). TCL, in turn, holds 100% stake in TCHFL.

 

Tata Sons also has personnel from its senior management on TCL's board. Tata Sons till date has infused capital of Rs 6,097 crore in TCL.

 

The TCL group, as the Tata group’s non-captive lending vehicle, is the primary financial services arm, and remains critical to the group, given the growth opportunities in this sector over the medium to long term. The TCL group is also strategically important to the Tata group because it caters to the funding requirements of various entities associated with the group, such as its suppliers, vendors and dealers.

 

The shared brand and infrastructural synergies with various Tata group companies strengthen the integration of the TCL group with the overall Tata group. Business synergies should increase further as TCL taps into the Tata group ecosystem as part of its growth strategy. Crisil Ratings believes that Tata Sons will continue to have majority ownership in, and management control of TCL and its subsidiaries, over the medium term.

 

Comfortable capitalisation to support medium term growth plans

The TCL group holds comfortable capitalisation, with consolidated networth (including minority interest) of Rs 50,469 crore as on September 30, 2026 as against Rs 47,107 crore as on March 31, 2026. The group’s capitalisation has been supported by regular infusion from its parent to support growth, coupled with healthy internal accruals and the same is expected going forward too. The reported consolidated gearing stood at 5.5 times as on September 30, 2026 as compared to 5.3 times as on March 31, 2026.  The capital adequacy of TCL (standalone) was comfortable at 17.3% as on September 30, 2026.

 

For TCHFL, as on September 30, 2026, the networth was Rs 12,437 crore and the gearing stood at 6.4 times (Rs 11,325 crore and 6.5 times as on March 31, 2026). The capital adequacy of TCHFL was comfortable, with total capital ratio of 17.56% as on September 30, 2026.

 

Crisil Ratings believes that the TCL group is adequately capitalised to absorb asset-side risks. However, the TCL Group may require external capital if book growth is higher than internal accruals.

 

Diversified resource profile

The TCL group has access to diversified funding mix ranging from capital market borrowings to bank loans. TCL has a diversified lender base for bank and institutional funding from private sector banks, foreign banks and PSBs/ financial institutions, and is raising NCD (non-convertible debentures) from a diversified set of investors. The group has also raised money through ECB (External Commercial Borrowings) route. As on September 30, 2026, the group held total borrowings of Rs 2,60,846 crore which comprised of term loans (39% share), NCDs and Tier II/Perpetual (38% share), working capital loans and commercial papers (9% share), external commercial borrowing (12% share) and from NHB (3% share). TCL and its subsidiaries can mobilise debt at competitive costs due to their association with the Tata group.

 

Comfortable asset quality metrics

TCL group reported an improvement in overall gross stage 3 assets (GS3) to 1.9% as on September 30, 2026 from 2.0% as on March 31, 2026. The provision coverage ratio stood at 58.6% as on September 30, 2026 and 56.2% as on March 31, 2026, translating into net stage 3 assets (NS3) of 0.8% as on September 30, 2026 and 0.9% as on March 31, 2026.

 

TCHFL reported improved GS3 ratio of 0.68% as on September 30, 2026 as against 0.73% as on March 31, 2026. The provision coverage ratio for TCHFL stood at 57.4% as on September 30, 2026 and was 54.8% as on March 31, 2026, translating to NS3 ratio of 0.29% as on September 30, 2026 from 0.33% as on March 31, 2026.

 

The group’s portfolio consisted of retail (56.7% share), SME (28.7% share) and corporate (14.6% share) segments, with the unsecured retail segment constituting 10.4% of net AUM as on September 30, 2026. Over the near-to-medium term, the company plans on increasing focus on the unsecured segment. Resultantly, the group’s ability to contain the slippages and maintain overall asset quality metrics as the portfolio continues to scale remains monitorable.

Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses

Moderate, albeit improving, earnings profile

The earnings profile of the group, as seen by TCL’s profit after tax (PAT) stood at Rs 3,460 crore for the first half of fiscal 2027, and was Rs 4,891 crore in fiscal 2026, was supported by improving total net income, operating expenses and credit costs. As a percentage of average managed assets, the total income (net of interest expenses) improved to 6.0% (annualised) for the first half of fiscal 2027 from 5.8% in fiscal 2026, while operating expenses improved to 2.1% (annualised) for the first half of fiscal 2027 from 2.2% in fiscal 2026 and credit costs also improved to  0.9% (annualised) for the first half of fiscal 2027 from 1.1% for fiscal 2026. Consequently, the return on average managed assets (RoMA), improved to 2.3% (annualised) for the first half of fiscal 2027 from 1.8% in fiscal 2026.

 

TCHFL reported standalone PAT of Rs 1,084 crore for the first half of fiscal 2027 and PAT of Rs 1,836 crore in fiscal 2026 translating to a stable return on managed assets (ROMA) of 2.4% (annualised) for the first half of fiscal 2027 from 2.5% in fiscal 2026. The group’s ability to improve profitability while controlling credit costs and sustain at healthier levels will remain monitorable.

Liquidity Superior

The asset liability management (ALM) of TCL and TCHFL as on September 30, 2026, shows no cumulative negative mismatches up to one year maturity bucket (the inflows include existing bank limits). In terms of liquidity, as on September 30, 2026, TCL group had total unencumbered liquidity of Rs. 15,287 crore (cash equivalents, liquid investments and unutilised working capital bank lines), which was sufficient to meet debt obligations (including interest) for the upcoming months. Furthermore, the group also has available undrawn term loans and ECB aggregating to Rs. 13,252 crore, as on September 30, 2026, which provide comfort.  

Outlook Stable

Crisil Ratings believes that the TCL group will remain highly strategically important to Tata Sons and will continue to benefit from the strong parent support over the medium term.

Rating Sensitivity factors

Downward factors

  • Downward revision in the credit rating of Tata Sons Pvt Ltd (Tata Sons)
  • Any change in the support philosophy of Tata Sons resulting in reduced support to the TCL group
  • Sharp deterioration in consolidated asset quality with consolidated gross stage 3 assets remaining above 6% and in-turn impacting capital and earnings

About the Company

TCL is the holding company for the financial services businesses of the TCL group and is registered with the Reserve Bank of India as a systemically important, non-deposit-taking, core investment company. TCL has a diversified product portfolio, with a presence in both the wholesale and retail finance segments. The consolidated AUM was Rs 3,08,030 as on September 30, 2026 and Rs 2,77,275 crore as on March 31, 2026. The fee-based services distribution of mutual funds and insurance products, stock broking and investment banking are offered through the wholly owned subsidiary, Tata Securities Ltd. TCHFL is engaged in mortgage finance, with loan portfolio of Rs 95,098 crore as on September 30, 2026 as compared to Rs 86,653 crore as on March 31, 2026.
