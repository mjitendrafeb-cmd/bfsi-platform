Detailed Rationale

Crisil Ratings has assigned its ‘Crisil AA+’ rating to Rs.2000 crore non convertible debentures of Aseem Infrastructure Finance Limited (AIFL) and placed it on ‘Rating Watch with Developing Implications’. Crisil Ratings has also continued its rating on the existing non convertible debentures of AIFL on ‘Rating Watch with Developing Implications'.

 

The rating was placed on watch on July 13, 2026, following announcement by AIFL regarding an agreement for proposed sale of its entire shareholding. The existing shareholders, National Investment and Infrastructure Fund (NIIF; 59.05%), Government of India (GoI; 30.95%) and Sumitomo Mitsui Banking Corporation (SMBC; 10%), will transfer their stakes to TPG Nicobar SG Pte. Ltd. (TPG Nicobar). TPG Nicobar is held by TPG Rise Climate Fund (70.2%) and Clydo Investments Pte Limited (29.8%), a wholly owned subsidiary of GIC Singapore. The transaction has been noted by the Board of AIFL and has received the requisite Competition Commission of India (CCI) approval and is now subject to approval from the Reserve Bank of India (RBI).

 

In the second step of the proposed sale transaction, ICICI Bank is expected to acquire a 5% stake in AIFL. The successful consummation of this transaction remains subject to requisite approvals.

 

The board and shareholders of AIFL have approved the sale of its entire stake in NIIF Infrastructure Finance Limited (NIIF IFL), Associate company of AIFL to National Investment and Infrastructure Fund II for ~Rs 1,750 crore.

 

Crisil Ratings will continue to track the progress of the sale transaction to TPG and remain in discussions with the company’s management to understand TPG’s support stance, commitment, strategic importance and long-term business and financial strategy for the company. The watch will be resolved once requisite regulatory approvals are in place. Upon resolution of the watch, the rating is unlikely to be downgraded in view of the expected support from TPG. 

 

Nevertheless, till the transaction is consummated, the rating will continue to factor in the significant benefits and financial flexibility derived from the current strong sponsors. The ratings also factor in the comfortable capitalisation of AIFL, its ability to raise funds at competitive rates, given its shareholding, and an experienced management team. These strengths are partially offset by modest, albeit growing, scale of operations.

 

AIFL was incorporated on May 23, 2019, and initiated lending operations in August 2020. With healthy growth, the company’s assets under management (AUM) stood at Rs 17,632 crore as on June 30, 2026, with around 77% exposure to operational projects, as against AUM of Rs 18,264 crore as on March 31, 2026 (Rs 15,431 crore as on March 31, 2025). In furtherance of the transaction, the company will down-sell its non-green exposures and the loan portfolio will be realigned towards the green financing strategy as per the mandate of TPG Rise Climate Fund upon consummation of the transaction.

Analytical Approach

Crisil Ratings has considered the standalone business and financial risk profiles of AIFL, and the expectation of support from sponsors, including GoI.

Key Rating Drivers - Strengths

Significant benefits and financial flexibility on account of strong sponsors

At present, AIFL continues to benefit from the expectation of strong support by the existing sponsors. As on June 30, 2026, Strategic Opportunities Fund (SOF) (managed by NIIF) had 59% equity stake in AIFL and the GoI had 31%, with the balance 10% held by SMBC. NIIF is a collaborative investment platform for Indian and international investors anchored by the GoI. It invests across asset classes, such as infrastructure, private equity and other diversified sectors in India through three funds: Master Fund, Fund of Funds and Strategic Opportunities Fund. GoI holds 49% stake in NIIF, and the remaining is held by domestic and global institutional investors. Currently, the board of directors of AIFL includes one nominee director from NIIF and two independent directors. Upon successful consummation of the transaction, the board composition may change.

 

Given the important role AIFL plays by offering funding assistance to infrastructure projects, it remains strategically important to GoI and therefore receives strong support. The strategic importance and support are reflected in the sizeable equity commitments of GoI across AIFL and NIIF IFL. AIFL also benefits from its sovereign ownership in being able to raise resources at competitive rates.

 

Crisil Ratings will monitor the progress of the stake sale to TPG Nicobar and will engage with the latter to understand its stance of support, commitment, strategic importance and long-term business and financial strategies for the company.

 

Comfortable capital position

Capitalisation is healthy, supported by the flexibility to raise additional capital and presence of strong sponsors. Classified as an NBFC-IFC (infrastructure finance company), AIFL has to maintain minimum Tier-I and overall capital adequacy ratio (CAR) of 10% and 15%, respectively. As on June 30, 2026, the Tier-I and CAR ratios stood at 17.3% and 19.4%, (16.8% and 18.7%, respectively, as on March 31, 2026), respectively, with networth around Rs 3,683 crore (Rs 3,614 as on March 31, 2026).

 

Gearing stood at 4.5 times as on June 30, 2026, and March 31, 2026, and is expected to reduce to below 3 times upon completion of the transaction given the expected increase in networth consequent to profit from sale of the stake in NIIF IFL, internal accrual and reduction in borrowing due to down selling of the loan book. However, gearing is expected to increase to around 5 times over the long term as the company grows its loan book. The capital position of AIFL will likely remain comfortable over the medium term.

 

Experienced management

The senior management team of AIFL comprises personnel with several years of experience in the infrastructure finance space and across critical business functions. Virender Pankaj, CEO, oversees the operations. He has over 30 years of experience in lending to sectors such as power, roads, social and industrial finance. The management has put in place strong risk management systems, processes and policies.

Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses

Moderate, albeit growing, scale of operations

AIFL commenced operations in August 2020 and has a moderately seasoned loan portfolio. Overall AUM stood at Rs 17,632 crore as on June 30, 2026, as against Rs 18,264 crore as on March 31, 2026 (15,431 crore as on March 31, 2025). As on June 30, 2026, the company’s portfolio was spread across 191 special purpose vehicles, with 77% exposure towards operational projects.

 

The company has started to downsize the non-green portfolio for the realignment of the loan book towards the green financing strategy of TPG Nicobar upon consummation of the transaction.

 

The company has added greenfield as well as brownfield projects in its portfolio in addition to growth in operational projects. However, operational projects will retain major share. A dedicated and experienced team and adequate risk management systems and processes will help the company grow its loan book responsibly.

 

AIFL had nil gross stage 3 assets (GS3) as on June 30, 2026. In addition, the company has not reported any days past due (dpd) since the start of operations. However, the asset quality will be monitorable over the long term, given the high concentration in the loan book and the inherent nature of the infrastructure funding business. Though exposure to risk will be lower in the initial stage - as has been the case until now - with focus on operational or near operational projects, as the company diversifies into under-construction projects, its ability to maintain adequate asset quality and successfully scale up the business will be monitorable.

Liquidity Strong

As per the structural asset liability management (ALM) statement as on July 31, 2026, AIFL had cumulative positive mismatch in buckets of up to one year. The liquidity of the company is comfortable with cash and liquid investments of Rs 1,060 crore (provisional) and unutilised bank lines of Rs 1,250 crore (Including CC/WCDL of Rs. 950 crore) as on July 31, 2026 which were sufficient to cover outflows (including operating expenditures) for the upcoming four months.

Rating sensitivity factors

Upward factors

  • Enhanced strategic importance to sponsor
  • Building a leading market position across diversified sectors, while maintaining healthy asset quality and improving the earnings profile on a sustained basis

 

Downward factors

  • Decline in the strategic importance to, or support received from the sponsors.
  • Significant weakening in asset quality and profitability exerting pressure on capitalisation, reflected in gearing increasing to, and remaining above, 5 times for a prolonged period.

About the Company

AIFL, incorporated in May 2019, is registered as a non-banking financial company-infrastructure finance company (NBFC-IFC) with the Reserve Bank of India. The company commenced operations from August 2020 and funds infrastructure projects. As on June 30, 2026, the company had AUM of Rs 17,632 crore with renewables projects accounting for ~84% of the loan book, other major sectors such as roads (15%) and smaller exposure to segments, such as transmission and logistics.

 

The company achieved profit after tax of Rs 74 crore on total income of Rs 458 crore for the first three months of fiscal 2027. For fiscal 2026, AIFL reported PAT of Rs 346 crore and total income of Rs 1,664 crore (PAT of Rs 262 crore and total income of Rs 1,466 crore).
