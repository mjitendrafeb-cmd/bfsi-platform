Detailed Rationale

Crisil Ratings has continued its rating on the existing long-term bank facilities and non-convertible debentures of Avendus Finance Private Limited (AFPL) on ‘Rating Watch with Positive Implications’ and has reaffirmed its 'Crisil A1+’ rating on the commercial paper.

 

The rating action follows the announcement made by Avendus Capital Pvt Ltd (ACPL; ‘Crisil A+/Watch Positive’) on December 17, 2025, as reported in a press release, that Redpoint Investments Pte Ltd., an affiliate of KKR, intends to divest its stake in the firm to Mizuho Securities Co. Ltd. (rated A/Stable/A-1 by S&P Global Ratings), a subsidiary of Mizuho Financial Group, Inc.

 

As a result of this transaction, Mizuho Securities will invest approximately Rs 5,800 crore in ACPL to acquire a stake of around 61.6% to 78.3%, currently held by KKR (approximately 63%), Avendus co-founder and executive vice chairman, Mr. Ranu Vohra (around 6%), and the remainder in the form of employee stock options. Upon completion, ACPL will become a consolidated subsidiary of Mizuho Securities Co. Ltd. ACPL will become a strategic partner of Mizuho Securities Co. Ltd.

 

As part of the transaction, Mr. Ranu Vohra will divest his shareholding but will remain in his current role until the deal is completed, which is expected to occur by October 2026, subject to various regulatory and statutory clearances, as stated by the management.

 

Avendus has received approvals from the stock exchanges, clearing corporations, and the Competition Commission of India in connection with the transaction. The approval process for the remaining licenses, including the non-banking financial company (NBFC) license are yet to come.

 

Mizuho Securities, established in July 1917, is an investment bank that delivers sophisticated services and optimal financial solutions that meet a broad array of client needs. The company is mainly into three lines of businesses: global investment banking business, global markets division and research (in–depth investment information). As on March 31, 2026, Mizuho Securities reported assets under management (AUM) of JPY 67,771 billion and a workforce of 6,479 employees.

 

The existing analytical approach factors in consolidated business and financial risk profiles of ACPL and its subsidiaries, including AFPL, given their integrated manner of operations, shared brand, common promoters, and shared management pool.

 

Upon completion of the transaction, the revised analytical approach will additionally take into account the potential benefits arising from Mizuho Securities Co. Ltd., the new parent company, and assess their impact on ACPL's credit profile. Crisil Ratings believes that upon resolution of the Watch, the rating is likely to move upward by 1 notch or more.

 

Crisil Ratings will monitor the progress of the proposed transaction. Until the completion of the transaction, Crisil Ratings’ assessment will remain based on the existing analytical approach and the standalone operating and financial profile of the Avendus group, which continues to operate as an independent entity.

 

Crisil Ratings will engage with Mizuho Securities' management to understand their strategy and business plan for India, as well as the expected support for ACPL. This will include aspects such as the final shareholding, strategic support, and expected ongoing and distress support levels. The rating watch will be resolved once the transaction is complete and Crisil Ratings has clarity on these aspects.

 

The ratings centrally factor in the expectation of strong support to AFPL from ACPL (ACPL; the flagship company of the Avendus group), as well as the experienced management, healthy capitalisation and conservative gearing policy. These strengths are partially offset by modest scale of operations and inherent vulnerability associated with the wholesale financing segment.

Analytical Approach

Crisil Ratings has assessed the standalone business and financial risk profiles of AFPL and factored in the support from ACPL, both on an ongoing basis and in case of distress. This is because AFPL and ACPL have extensive business and operational linkages, and a common brand. Crisil Ratings believes ACPL will continue to provide support to AFPL, considering the strategic importance of the latter, the shared brand name and 100% shareholding of ACPL in AFPL.

Key Rating Drivers - Strengths

  • Expectation of support from the parent, ACPL:

AFPL is an important subsidiary for ACPL, as it undertakes financing business for the Avendus group and supports the group’s product offering and revenue profile. AFPL had a loan book of Rs 1,395 crore (including loans against shares and structured finance) as on June 30, 2026. There is strong operational and managerial integration between AFPL and the Avendus group. ACPL shares its strong technology platform and risk management practices with AFPL. Given the 100% shareholding, Crisil Ratings believes ACPL will provide both funding and capital assistance to AFPL, if required. The extensive operational, managerial and financial linkages, along with 100% shareholding and shared brand also imply support from ACPL to AFPL.

 

  • Experienced management:

The Avendus group has an experienced management team to run and scale up operations. The long track record and extensive experience of management in the financial services space have helped establish the group’s track record in the lending business since inception. The management has scaled up AUM to nearly Rs 94,639 crore as on June 30, 2026, on a consolidated basis (comprising Rs 87,488 crore of wealth AUM, Rs 5,756 crore of asset management AUM and Rs 1,395 crore of wholesale lending AUM) while maintaining adequate asset quality amidst macroeconomic challenges. ACPL, being the 100% shareholder, is likely to support and strengthen AFPL’s systems, processes and governance framework, given its track record of supporting growth of a diversified set of financial services businesses in India and abroad.

 

  • Adequate capitalization:

With standalone networth (provisional) of Rs 1,026 crore and capital adequacy of 58.10% as on June 30, 2026, AFPL is adequately positioned to support growth over the medium term. The gearing policy is conservative, and the management intends to keep gearing below 3 times for the lending business (AFPL) over the medium term on a steady-state basis (0.5 time as on June 30, 2026).

Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses

  • Modest scale of operations:

AFPL commenced lending operations in fiscal 2017 in the wholesale financing segment and had a loan book of around Rs 1,395 crore as on June 30, 2026 (Rs 1,231 crore as on March 31, 2026). AFPL provides structured finance (SF) loans (~64%) and loans against securities (~36%) for mid-market corporate and individual clients of the Avendus group. The loan book growth has been typically modest, due to factors such as high prepayment as well as the management’s business strategy to supplement growth through both balance sheet exposure and through the alternate investment fund (AIF). Strong relationships of the promoters and their understanding of the business dynamics of mid-corporates support the lending business.

 

  • Inherent vulnerability associated with the wholesale financing segment:

Given the wholesale nature of the business, asset quality remains vulnerable to slippages even in 1-2 accounts. The gross non-performing asset (GNPA) ratio was high at 6.3% as on March 31, 2023, on account of one exposure (amounting to Rs 83.4 crore) slipping into NPA category. The account was written off later in fiscal 2024. As a result, there were nil gross NPAs as on June 30, 2026.

 

The earnings profile is supported by multiple income sources — interest income and syndication income. AFPL reported a profit after tax (PAT) of Rs 75 crore on a total income (net of interest expense) of Rs 166 crore for fiscal 2026, against Rs 94 crore and Rs 162 crore, respectively, in fiscal 2025. Return on managed assets (RoMA) has declined to 4.6% in fiscal 2026 from 5.8% in previous fiscal, due to increase in employee expenses. Further, the company earned PAT of Rs 21 crore on a total income (net of interest expense) of Rs 39 crore, on provisional basis in the first quarter of fiscal 2027 translating to an annualised RoMA of 5.3%. The company had incurred credit costs in fiscals 2022 and 2023 towards the slippage of one account. On a steady state basis, the RoMA is expected to normalise but remain healthy over the medium term.

 

Overall, the ability to profitably scale up the business while managing asset quality remains monitorable.

Liquidity Strong

Liquidity of the group is strong, with unencumbered cash and bank balance, mutual funds, liquid investments and fixed deposits amounted to Rs 829 crore and unutilized lines amounted to Rs 50 crore as on August 31, 2026, against which debt repayments aggregating to Rs 308 crore up to February 2027. Liquidity is sufficient in view of the current scale of operations and diversified revenue streams (fee-based businesses contribute to most of the revenue).

Rating sensitivity factors

Upward factors

  • Upgrade by 1 notch in the ratings on the parent, ACPL
  • Significant improvement in market position of the financial services businesses, while maintaining asset quality and earnings profile

 

Downward factors

  • Downgrade in the ratings of ACPL by 1 notch or material change in the shareholding or support philosophy of the parent impacting the quantum and timing of support
  • Deterioration in asset quality with elevated gross NPAs over an extended period, thereby impacting profitability

About the Company

AFPL is a wholly owned subsidiary of ACPL, and offers financing products such as promoter funding, corporate finance, customised structured debt solutions, and acquisition finance. The company had outstanding loans of about Rs 1,395 crore as on June 30, 2026 (Rs 1,231 crore as on March 31, 2026).

 

AFPL reported PAT of Rs 75 crore on total income (net of interest expense) of Rs 166 crore for fiscal 2026, against Rs 94 crore and Rs 162 crore, respectively, for fiscal 2025. RoMA declined to 4.6% in fiscal 2026, from 5.8% for the previous fiscal.
