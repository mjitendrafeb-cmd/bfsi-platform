Detailed Rationale

Crisil Ratings has reaffirmed its ratings on the bank facilities of Sri Vignesh Yarns Private Limited (SVYPL; part of the SV group) at ‘Crisil BBB/Negative/Crisil A3+’.

 

The ratings factor in the anticipated recovery in year-on-year operating performance in fiscal 2026. Although revenue growth would remain flat in fiscal 2026, operating margins are expected to recover to 10.5-11% supported by lower procurement prices and benefit of solar capacities added earlier.

 

The ratings continue to reflect the extensive experience of the promoters in the textile industry, the strong market position of the group and its healthy capital structure. These strengths are partially offset by moderation in debt protection metrics, working capital-intensive operations and susceptibility of operating performance to volatility in cotton prices.

Analytical Approach

Crisil Ratings has combined the business and financial risk profiles of Shri Siddhivinayaga Tex India Private Limited (SSVTIPL), SVYPL, SS Spinning Mills (SSSM) and Prabhu Spinning Mills Private Limited (PSMPL). This is because all four entities, collectively referred to as the SV group, are in similar businesses, under common management, and have significant operational and financial linkages.

 

Please refer Annexure - List of Entities Consolidated, which captures the list of entities considered and their analytical treatment of consolidation.

Key Rating Drivers - Strengths

Extensive experience of the promoters, and strong market position of the SV group: The group has established market position in the textile industry, supported by the three-decade-long experience of the promoters, longstanding supplier and customer relationships, and established track record in the cotton yarn industry. Consequently, revenue was healthy at Rs 1,117 crore in fiscal 2025 (Rs 1,021 crore in fiscal 2024). Strong market presence and relationships with customers will help in sustaining the business risk profile of the group.
 

Comfortable capital structure: Capital structure was healthy, as reflected in gearing and networth of 1.15 times and Rs 509 crore, respectively, as on March 31, 2025. 

Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses

Moderation in debt protection metrics: Debt protection metrics were subdued, as indicated by net cash accrual to adjusted debt and interest coverage ratios of 0.06 time and 1.92 times, respectively, in fiscal 2025. Improvement in the debt protection metrics will be a key monitorable.
 

Working capital-intensive operations: Gross current assets were large at 266 days as on March 31, 2025, due to inventory of 120 days and stretched receivables. Cotton, the key raw material, is available only during the crop season, from October to March, and the group procures its requirement during this period. As a result, inventory remains sizeable. Receivables stood at 93 days as on March 31, 2025, mainly on account of large credit period provided to customers by PSMPL. Debtors above six months rose to Rs 51 crore in fiscal 2025 from Rs 14 crore in fiscal 2024 for PSMPL. While other units of the group maintain receivables largely under six months at above 97% of their debtor levels, the group’s overall debtors under six months reduced to 82% in fiscal 2025 owing to stress in PSMPL. Improvement in the working capital cycle will be a key monitorable.
 

Susceptibility of operating performance to volatility in cotton prices: Operating performance remains susceptible to volatility in raw material prices. Intense competition limits the group’s ability to pass on any steep increase in raw material prices to customers. Any change in customer preference will also affect performance.

Liquidity Adequate

Bank limit utilisation is moderate at around 75 percent for the past twelve months ended February 2026. Cash accrual are expected to be over Rs 60 crore which are sufficient against term debt obligation of Rs 42-48 crore over the medium term.

 

Current ratio are healthy at 1.80 times on March31, 2025 The promoters are likely to extend support in the form of equity and unsecured loans to meet its working capital requirements and repayment obligations.

Outlook Negative

Crisil Ratings believes any potential impact on the financial risk profile of the SV group owing to subdued profitability will constrain the credit risk profile.

Rating sensitivity factors

Upward factors:

  • Improvement in revenue and operating margin of more than 9% leading to higher cash accrual
  • Sustenance of healthy financial risk profile

 

Downward factors:

  • Decline in revenue or fall in profitability to less than 8% leading to lower cash accrual
  • Deterioration in financial risk profile or liquidity.

About the Company

SSVTIPL, incorporated in 1983 in Tiruppur, Tamil Nadu, manufactures hosiery yarn and compact yarn of 10-60s and grey fabric.

About the Group

The SV group comprises SSVTIPL, SVYPL, SSSM and PSMPL. PSMPL manufactures normal and open-ended yarn. SVYPL and SSSM are in similar businesses. The daily operations are managed by Mr R T Sivakumar (Managing Director) and his son, Mr S Saravana Sudan. The group inherited PSMPL in fiscal 2023.
