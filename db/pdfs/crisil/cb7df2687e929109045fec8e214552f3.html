Detailed Rationale

Crisil Ratings has assigned its 'Crisil A1’ rating to the Rs 100 crore commercial paper programme of Earlysalary Services Private Limited (EarlySalary)

 

EarlySalary and Social Worth Technologies Ltd (SWTL; the entity housing the technology platform of EarlySalary) are together referred to as the group.

 

The rating reflects the healthy capitalisation metrics of the company along with its consistent track record in raising equity, its scalable business model, equipped with technology for end-to-end operations, and diversified resource profile. These strengths are partially offset by inherent vulnerability of asset quality metrics associated with unsecured personal loans along with sustenance of improvement in the earnings profile will be key monitorable.

 

EarlySalary is a wholly-owned subsidiary of SWTL, is a technology platform running under the brand name – fibe, through which loans are originated. The company focused on small-ticket salary-advance loans earlier, with tenor less than six months. However, gradually the company has shifted to longer tenure loans over the last few years. Starting 2021, EarlySalary started offering personal loans with monitored end-use, labeled as ‘purpose-driven financing’. These loans are currently offered to fund education, healthcare, insurance premiums and residential rooftop solar projects, and are originated at the point of purchase, with disbursements made directly to merchants or service providers.

 

The group maintains a healthy capitalisation profile, supported by equity raise at regular intervals. Net worth stood at Rs 2,186 crore, while gearing was comfortable at 1.6 times as on March 31, 2026. The in-house technology platform supports end-to-end integration across loan origination, underwriting, disbursements and collections. This has enabled the company to rapidly scale up and reach assets under management (AUM) of Rs 8,603 crore as on March 31, 2026, from Rs 1,963 crore as on March 31, 2023, translating into a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of ~64% over the three fiscals.

 

However, the company's portfolio remains exposed to inherent asset quality risk, associated with unsecured consumer lending. Nevertheless, adjusted 90+ days past due (dpd; including preceding 12-months write-offs) have improved to 4.1% as on March 31, 2026 compared to 7.4% as on March 31, 2025. Profit after tax and return on managed assets (RoMA) improved to Rs 257 crore and 3.4%, respectively, for fiscal 2026, compared to Rs 113 crore and 2.2%, respectively, for fiscal 2025, aided by improving operating leverage and lower credit cost. However, sustained improvement in asset quality, along with stable earnings as the portfolio scales up, remains key monitorable.

Analytical Approach

Crisil Ratings has evaluated the consolidated credit and financial risk profile of the group i.e. SWTL and EarlySalary, considering the significant operational and managerial linkages between the companies. Additionally, all equity raises are done at the parent company i.e. SWTL, part of which is infused in EarlySalary.

Key Rating Drivers - Strengths

Healthy capitalisation metrics

Capitalisation metrics are healthy, supported by regular capital infusion and steady internal accrual. The group reported networth of Rs 2,186 crore as on March 31, 2026, compared to Rs 1,706 crore as on March 31, 2025. Gearing was also comfortable at 1.6 times as on March 31, 2026, as against 1.1 times a year earlier. On a steady-state basis, gearing is expected to stay below 3.5 times.

 

The group has been able to raise capital at periodic intervals from marquee investors including TPG Inc (through The Rise Fund), Norwest Capital LLC, Eight Roads Ventures and International Finance Corporation (IFC). The latest capital raise was in December 2025, wherein Rs 215 crore was raised from IFC. So far, the group has cumulatively raised Rs 1,747 crore since inception.

 

Crisil Ratings also notes that SWTL has filed a draft red herring prospectus for an initial public offering, comprising a fresh issue of up to Rs 750 crore. Of the proposed proceeds, Rs 562.6 crore is likely to be infused into EarlySalary to augment its capital base and support onward lending. The proposed capital infusion, if completed, should strengthen the group’s capitalisation profile and support medium-term growth.

 

Scalable digital lending platform supported by purpose-driven finance offerings

EarlySalary mainly offers unsecured personal loans to young salaried professionals. The company was earlier focused on small-ticket salary-advance loans with tenure less than six months. Over the past few years, it has gradually shifted towards higher-ticket and longer-tenure personal loans with average ticket size of around Rs. 80,000 and average tenure of 16 months. It has also diversified into purpose-driven loans with pre-defined end use. In August 2025, the company also launched travel and e-commerce checkout financing. Currently, around 50% of the new customers are acquired through purpose-driven loans. 

 

The company follows a fully digital lending model, with loan origination undertaken through the fibe mobile application and website, owned and operated by SWTL. The in-house technology platform supports the entire lending process, including customer acquisition, credit assessment, underwriting, disbursement, servicing and collections.

 

With gradual increase in loan tenure, disbursements have recorded a CAGR of ~43% over fiscals 2023 to 2026, to reach Rs 15,727 crore from Rs 5,409 crore. With increased disbursements, the group’s AUM has also grown by ~64% (CAGR) to Rs 8,603 crore as on March 31, 2026, from Rs 1,963 crore as on March 31, 2023. Personal loans formed ~77% of the AUM as on March 31, 2026, while purpose-driven finance constituted the remaining 23%.

 

The recent growth in AUM has been driven by a high proportion of repeat customers, increasing ticket sizes and longer loan tenures, and expansion of purpose-driven products, providing cross-sell opportunities for personal loans. Further, considering the scalable business model the AUM growth is expected to remain healthy over the medium term.

 

Diversified resource profile

EarlySalary’s resource profile is diversified across products and lenders. The lender base has expanded with more banks and other financial institutions coming onboard. Of the total external outstanding borrowing of Rs 3,318 crore as on March 31, 2026, ~45% was in the form of term loans and working capital, ~7% pass-through certificates, ~47% non-convertible debentures and ~1% from CPs. During the fiscal 2026, the weighted average incremental cost of borrowing came down to 10.8% from 11.7% for fiscal 2025.

Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses

Inherent vulnerability in asset quality owing to risks associated with the asset class

The group extends personal loans, which remains vulnerable to inherent risk in the unsecured asset segment.

 

Nonetheless, asset quality has improved with 90+ dpd declining to 1.1% as on March 31, 2026, from 2.5% as on March 31, 2025 (1.9% as on March 31, 2024). Adjusted 90+ dpd (including preceding 12 months write-off) improved to ~4.0%, as compared to 7.4% over the same period. This improvement is on account of increased focus on longer-tenure and larger-ticket loans and increasing share of purpose-driven financing in the overall portfolio.

 

However, given the recent strong AUM growth, the company’s ability to sustain comfortable asset quality as the portfolio seasons remains key monitorable.

 

Improving earnings profile; sustenance to be monitored

Profit after tax and return on managed assets (RoMA) improved to Rs 257 crore and 3.4%, respectively, for fiscal 2026, compared to Rs 113 crore and 2.2%, respectively, for fiscal 2025.

 

This was supported by better operating leverage and moderation in credit cost. Operating expenses declined to 6.6% of average managed assets from 8.0%, while credit cost moderated to 5.5% from 9.7%, led by improvement in overall asset quality.

 

However, total income (net of interest expense) moderated to 17.2% of average managed assets in fiscal 2026, from 20.2% in fiscal 2025, mainly due to the increasing share of relatively lower-yielding purpose-driven finance and the shift towards higher-ticket and longer-tenure personal loans. The impact was partly offset by decline in incremental cost of borrowing, lower credit cost and by operational efficiencies.

 

Growing share of purpose-driven finance should offer higher cross-selling opportunities for personal loans and also aid acquisition cost and operating efficiency. However, the group’s ability to sustain its earning profile while scaling up operations remains key monitorable.

Liquidity Adequate

Asset-liability mismatch of EarlySalary remains comfortable with positive mismatches across all maturity buckets as on June 30, 2026. As on the same date, the company had unencumbered cash and equivalents of ~Rs 547 crore and unutilised working capital bank limit of Rs 54 crore, which should suffice to cover more than two months of debt obligations and estimated operating expenses.

Rating sensitivity factors

Upward factors:

  • Increase in scale of operations while maintaining profitability
  • Sustenance of asset quality metrics with adjusted 90+ dpd of around 3% on a steady state basis

 

Downward factors:

  • Increase in steady state gearing to above 4 times
  • Sustained deterioration in asset quality thereby impacting profitability

About the Company

EarlySalary is registered as a non-banking financial company with the Reserve Bank of India, and is engaged in providing unsecured personal loans to professionals and salaried employees. The company also provides purpose-driven financing across education, healthcare, insurance premiums and residential rooftop solar.

 

EarlySalary is a wholly owned subsidiary of SWTL, which was incorporated in October 2015, by the founders, Akshay Mehrotra and Ashish Goyal. SWTL operates the group’s technology platform, fibe, which supports digital loan origination, underwriting, disbursement, servicing and collections. The group follows a branchless digital lending model and serves customers across India. Consolidated AUM stood at Rs 8,603 crore as on March 31, 2026. The group reported a profit after tax of Rs 257 crore in fiscal 2026.
