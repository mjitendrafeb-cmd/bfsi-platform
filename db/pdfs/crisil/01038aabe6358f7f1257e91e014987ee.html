Detailed Rationale

Crisil Ratings has reaffirmed its ‘Crisil BBB+/Stable’ rating on the bank facilities and non convertible debentures of Namdev Finvest Private Limited (Namdev).

 

Crisil Ratings has withdrawn its rating on non-convertible debentures (NCDs) amounting to Rs 30 crore on redemption. Crisil Ratings has received independent confirmation that these instruments are fully redeemed. Crisil Ratings has also withdrawn its rating on non convertible debentures (NCDs) amounting to Rs 5.21 crore, proposed bank loan facilities amounting to Rs 250.22 crore and commercial paper amounting to Rs 50 crore (on confirmation from independent auditor that limit is not used) and upon client confirmation and receipt of requisite documentation. The rating is withdrawn in line with Crisil Ratings policy on withdrawal of ratings.

 

The ratings continue to reflect the company’s healthy capitalisation metrics backed by timely equity raises and internal accrual and comfortable earnings profile. These strengths are partially offset by high, albeit improving, geographical concentration as well as inherent vulnerability of asset quality metrics given the segment of operation.

 

Namdev’s capital position is supported by net worth of Rs 560 crore and comfortable leverage of 3.1 times as on June 30, 2026. The company also reported strong earnings with profit after tax (PAT) of Rs 54 crore, which translates to return on average managed assets (RoMA) of 2.5% for fiscal 2026, In the first quarter of fiscal 2027, the reported PAT and RoMA were at Rs 14 crore and 2.3% respectively.

 

While there has been reduction in the geographic concentration in the portfolio over the recent fiscals, concentration is still high, with the top 3 states accounting for 93% of the portfolio assets under management (AUM) as on June 30, 2026, with Rajasthan contributing 66%. The ratings also factor the inherent vulnerability of the asset segment, albeit metrics have been relatively stable for Namdev with 90+dpd (adjusted including 12-month write-offs) of 2.8% as on June 30, 2026, against 3.0% as on March 31, 2026, and 2.1% as on March 31, 2025. However, considering that portfolio has grown at a healthy CAGR of ~41% in AUM over the past three fiscals, track record of sustenance of asset quality with scale up in operations and especially with growth of portfolio outside of the historically stronghold state of Rajasthan remains to be seen. Sustenance of earnings with continued growth also remains a key monitorable.

Analytical Approach

Crisil Ratings has considered the standalone business, financial and management risk profiles of Namdev for arriving at the ratings

Key Rating Drivers - Strengths

Healthy capitalisation

Capitalisation metrics of the entity remain healthy with net worth at Rs.560 crore and gearing at 3.1 times as on June 30, 2026, and Rs 547 crores and 3.2 times, respectively, as on March 31, 2026 (Rs 460 crore and 3.0 times, respectively, as on March 31, 2025). Overall capital adequacy ratio also remained comfortable at 28.9% as on June 30, 2026, 29.4% as on March 31, 2026, and 30.2% as on March 31, 2025. Capitalisation metrics have been supported by regular equity infusion and healthy internal accruals. The company has raised ~ Rs. 385 since inception out of which ~Rs. 22 crore was infused in fiscal 2026 and Rs 0.05 crore in first quarter of fiscal 2027. Crisil Ratings expects capitalisation to remain healthy, supported by internal accruals. On a steady state basis, the gearing is expected to remain below 5 times. 

 

Comfortable earnings profile

The overall earnings profile remains comfortable as the company reported net profit of Rs 54 crore in fiscal 2026 as against Rs 41 crore in fiscal 2025. Furthermore, it reported profits of Rs 14 crore in the first quarter of fiscal 2027.

 

Net interest margin (NIM) margins have been steady at 8.8% for fiscal 2025 and 2026, with marginal increase to 9% for the first quarter of fiscal 2027. While operating expenses had decreased from 5.4% in fiscal 2025 to 5.2% in fiscal 2026, it has shown uptick 5.5% for Q1 fiscal 2027. Credit costs has decreased from 1% in fiscal 2025 to 0.9% in fiscal 2026, it rose marginally to 1.1% in the first quarter of fiscal 2027. As a result, return on managed assets (ROMA) of the company increased from 2.3% in fiscal 2025 to 2.5% in fiscal 2026 though moderated to 2.3% for the first quarter of fiscal 2027. The company’s ability to maintain comfortable earnings profile whilst scaling up operations will remain monitorable.

Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses

High, albeit improving, geographical concentration

Namdev’s AUM grew at a healthy growth rate of ~23% during fiscal 2026 to reach Rs 1750 crore from Rs 1420 crore after fiscal 2025. This further grew to Rs 1831 crore as on June 30, 2026. Namdev mainly focuses on MSME loans that account for ~93% of the portfolio followed by EV & solar loans (2%), two-wheeler loans (2%), wholesale lending (2%), and LCV/HCV/MUV (1%) as of June 30,2026.

 

Namdev has a network of 137 branches as on June 30, 2026 (131 as of March 2026) and has major concentration in Rajasthan (66%), Gujarat (15%), Madhya Pradesh (13%) and Uttar Pradesh (5%). In the last few years, the company has diversified its geographical composition and currently has a presence in 9 states - Rajasthan, Gujarat, Madhya Pradesh, Uttar Pradesh, Haryana, Bihar, Uttarakhand, Punjab, Delhi and recently started lending in Odisha. However, Rajasthan continued to dominate the majority of the portfolio with share of ~66% as of June 30, 2026. Going forward, the ability of the company to further reduce geographical concentration will remain monitorable.

 

Inherent vulnerability of asset quality metrics given the segment of operation:

Namdev has shown relatively stable asset quality performance so far. 90+ days past due (dpd) ratio stood at 2.2% as on June 30, 2026, compared to 2.5% as of March 31, 2026 and 1.8% as of March 31, 2025. Additionally, 90+dpd adjusted (including last 12 months write-offs) stands at 2.8% as of June 30, 2026 compared to 3% as of March 31, 2026 and 2.1% as of March 31, 2025. While, the collection efficiency has been healthy, averaging 97.8% over the last 12 months ending June 2026.

 

The company has put in place adequate underwriting practices and risk management practices where the credit team personally visits customers to gather essential information about their financial standing, collateral value, employment details and repayment capacity. This along with the information obtained from credit bureaus and the recorded loan details helps the team make informed decisions regarding loan approvals, interest rates, and repayment terms. Nevertheless, the asset quality metrics of the company remain inherently vulnerable to slippage given the segment of operation.  Moreover, as the company scales up its operations in the newer geographies, its ability to manage asset quality metrics will remain a key rating sensitivity factor.

Liquidity Adequate

The liquidity profile of the company remains adequate with positive cumulative mismatches across all buckets, as on June 30, 2026, and March 31, 2026. Also, on August 31, 2026, company had unencumbered liquidity of Rs 412 crore in form cash and cash equivalents, investments in liquid securities and unutilized CC/OD limits, against which it had total cash outflow of ~Rs 212 crore for the next 2 months.

Outlook Stable

Crisil Ratings believes Namdev Finvest will continue to maintain healthy capitalisation metrics and earnings profile. However, asset quality performance as the portfolio scales up will remain a key monitorable.

Rating sensitivity factors

Upward Factors:

  • Sustained improvement in asset quality metrics and sustenance of comfortable earnings profile whilst scaling up operations
  • Capitalisation metrics continue to remain comfortable, with gearing remaining under 4-5 times

 

Downward Factors:

  • Any adverse movement in asset quality with 90+dpd increasing beyond 4% and earnings profile of the company getting impacted.
  • Stress in capitalisation metrics with significant increase in gearing while scaling up the portfolio.

About the Company

Namdev Finvest Private Limited (Namdev Finvest) was incorporated as a private limited company under the Companies Act, 1956, on April 11, 1997. It is registered with the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) as a Non-Deposit Taking Non-Banking Financial Company (NBFC). In 2013, it was acquired and re-registered with the RBI in Jaipur. It has a network of 172 branches as of June 30, 2026, and operates in the states of Rajasthan, Gujarat, Madhya Pradesh, Uttar Pradesh, Haryana, Bihar, Uttarakhand, Punjab and Delhi.  The AUM of the company stood at Rs 1831 crore as on June 30, 2026. Company reported net profit of Rs 14 crore on a total income of Rs 110 crore for the first quarter of fiscal 2027 as against net profit of Rs 54 crore on a total income of Rs 386 crore for fiscal 2026.
