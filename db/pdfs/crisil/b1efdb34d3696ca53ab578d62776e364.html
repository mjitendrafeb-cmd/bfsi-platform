Detailed Rationale

Crisil Ratings has assigned its 'Crisil A-/Stable' rating to Rs 100 crore non-convertible debentures (NCDs) of Equirus Securities Private Limited (ESPL; part of the Equirus group[1]) and has reaffirmed its ratings on the existing long term bank loan facilities of the company.

 

The ratings continue to factor in the extensive experience of the senior management and presence of high pedigree investors, established track record of the company in the institutional broking segment and its expanding market presence in non-broking segments. These strengths are partially offset by the group’s moderate, though stable, profitability and inherent exposure to competitive intensity and uncertainties in the capital-market-related businesses, including regulatory changes.

 

The group, through ESPL, has an established track record of offering broking services to institutional clients with its research services covering over 300 companies across various sectors, including IT, pharmaceuticals, and financial services.

 

The investment banking vertical (housed in Equirus Capital Ltd [ECL]) focuses on Equity Capital Market transactions including mid-market initial public offerings (IPOs), qualified institutional placements (QIPs), Offer for Sale (OFS), Preferential Issues etc. and has been growing its market share through the debt capital market and advisory services for mergers and acquisitions and advisory business. During fiscal 2026, the value of deals managed under the investment banking arm cumulated to Rs 22,370 crore whereas the same for the period between April to August 2026 was Rs 5,810 crore (provisional).

 

For the wealth management business (housed in Equirus Wealth Pvt Ltd [EWPL] and its subsidiaries), the AUM has increased significantly to Rs 27,365 crore as on March 31, 2026, from Rs 9,961 crore as on March 31, 2025, driven by successful acquisition and integration of Sapient Finserv, Symphonia Wealth and Credence Family Office (Credence), in line with the company’s growth plans. Out of the total AUM, Sapient Finserv and Symphonia Wealth together accounted for a dominant share of 45%, while EWPL's organically sourced AUM contributed 42%; the balance was attributed to Credence.

 

Against the steady growth and diversification in business, the group’s capital position remains adequate. On a consolidated basis, networth stood at Rs 413 crore (excluding minority interest of Rs. 168.5 crore) as on March 31, 2026, compared to Rs 264 crore, a year ago. Gearing was 0.6 time and 0.2 time on the respective dates. Provisional networth and on-book gearing stood at Rs 421 crore and 0.7 time, respectively, as on June 30, 2026.

 

Capitalisation has been supported by equity infusion from high-pedigree investors (late Rakesh Jhunjhunwala, Federal Bank and Amicus Capital) over time. On a standalone basis, ESPL had liquid networth of Rs 90 crore on March 31, 2026, up from Rs 62 crore a year back.

 

Further, total income was Rs 413 crore for fiscal 2026 – of this, broking income constituted 33%, fee income from investment banking business (39%) and income from wealth management business formed the balance. Post fiscal 2022, the revenue mix has been diversifying with an increasing focus on recurring revenue, and the same is expected to sustain in the medium term. Consolidated profit after tax (PAT) stood at Rs 82 crore for fiscal 2026, as against Rs 98 crore for the previous fiscal. Corresponding return on equity was 24.0%, as against 46.5% for the respective years.

[1] Equirus group, includes ESPL, Equirus Capital Ltd (ECL) – Holding Company, Equirus Wealth Pvt Ltd (EWPL), Equirus Finance Pvt Ltd (EFPL), Credence Family office Pvt Ltd (Credence), Sapient Finserv Pvt Ltd, Symphonia Wealth Pvt Ltd and Associate concern - Equirus Raghnall Insurance Broking Pvt Ltd.

 

Analytical Approach

To arrive at the ratings on debt facilities of Equirus Securities Pvt Ltd (ESPL), the business and financial risk profiles of ESPL have been combined with other group entities – Equirus Capital Ltd (ECL), Equirus Wealth Pvt Ltd (EWPL), Equirus Securities Pvt Ltd (ESPL), Equirus Finance Pvt Ltd (EFPL), Credence Family Office Pvt Ltd and associate concern - Equirus Raghnall Insurance Broking Pvt Ltd, together referred to as Equirus group. This is because these companies have strong operational and financial linkages.

Key Rating Drivers - Strengths

Extensive experience of senior management and presence of high pedigree investors

The promoter, Ajay Rameshchandra Garg has over two decades of experience in carrying out and leading capital market-related businesses. He is a seasoned investment banker and prior to setting up the Equirus group, he led the private equity advisory desk at DSP Merrill Lynch, where he worked for six years after completing his MBA from Indian Institute of Management (Ahmedabad) in 1996. He is actively involved in strategic decision making and day-to-day operations of the group. Over time, the senior management team across businesses, has also been strengthened through onboarding of experienced professionals from renowned domestic and international capital market houses. The promoters and the top management have witnessed several cycles in the capital markets business and over time, have spearheaded the group’s diversification into related businesses such as investment banking and wealth management. They have also been instrumental in shoring up the group’s capital position, by attracting high-pedigree investors. The investor base comprises marquee names such as Rekha Jhunjhunwala (wife of late  Rakesh Jhunjhunwala [10.10% shareholding]), Federal Bank (8.29%) and Amicus Capital (10.06%) as of March 31, 2026. Apart from a seasoned leadership team, the company’s middle management team has also remained stable with limited attrition across verticals.

 

Established track record in the institutional broking segment

The group has established its track record in the institutional broking segment, through ESPL, which was incorporated in 2010. Through this entity, the group also provides research coverage on nearly 300 companies across sectors, including IT, pharma and financial services. Basis overall volume traded in fiscal 2025 on Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) and National Stock Exchange (NSE), its average market share on total market volume in the broking segment stood at 0.005% for the past three fiscals. The group had 870 clients as on June 30, 2026, of which ~47% were active.

 

The group’s market share in the total cash market segment was flat at 0.58% for fiscal 2026 vis-à-vis 0.51% a year ago, whereas that in the F&O segment moderated marginally to 0.0036% from 0.004% for the same period, in line with the broader market trend due to muted participation of foreign institutional investors in the capital market in the recent past (. The institutional equities team has more than 90 people, including 30–40 research analysts, 12 sales personnel, and 18–20 people in trading and back office.

 

While the institutional equity business is inherently exposed to volatility in capital markets, the group’s established track record and steady clientele should help offset part of the volatility and support the overall market position. Further, the group’s inter-company synergies are expected to aid cross-selling opportunities and support growth in emerging businesses as well.

 

Expanding market presence in the non-broking segments; sustenance in business diversity remains key

Apart from its established presence in the institutional broking space, other businesses of the Equirus group, which have commenced relatively later, have also gained tangible traction.

 

The investment banking business (housed in Equirus Capital Ltd [ECL]) incorporated in 2011, has been growing in the mid-market space through IPOs, QIPs, private placements and block deals. In fiscal 2026, cumulative value of transactions managed under the investment banking vertical was Rs 22,370 crore as against Rs 21,734 crore for the previous fiscal. Despite lower aggregate transaction value in fiscal 2026, the company generated higher revenue, supported by improved pricing prospects, better deal monetisation and higher share of advisory-led engagements.

 

AUM in the wealth management business rose to Rs 27,365 crore as on March 31, 2026, from Rs 9,961 crore as on March 31, 2025, aided by inorganic expansion via successful acquisition and integration of Sapient Finserv, Symphonia Wealth and Credence Family Office (Credence) – which was in line with the earlier expectations of Crisil Ratings.

 

While the group has a modest  market share based on deals managed and AUM, respectively, vis-à-vis other domestic peers, these businesses exhibit steady growth and impart diversity to the overall revenue profile.

 

Furthermore, the group continues to focus on strengthening diversification beyond its core businesses by expanding into adjacent segments, including alternative investment funds (AIFs), custodian services, real estate fund, and non-bank finance company (NBFC) operations, which should offer additional growth avenues and diversify the earnings profile over the medium term.

 

While these segments are expected to grow in cohesion and benefit from synergies over the medium term, the ability to garner a meaningful market share and presence in the wealth management and investment banking segments remains monitorable.

Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses

Moderate, though stable, earnings profile

The earnings profile of Equirus Group remained moderate, though stable, in fiscal 2026, partly reflecting the inherent nature of active businesses that entail long gestation periods before achieving meaningful scale and profitability. The consolidated profit stood at Rs 82 crore in fiscal 2026 as against Rs 98 crore in the previous fiscal. Correspondingly, return on equity (RoE) was 24.0% and 46.5%, respectively, over the same period.

 

While earnings of ECL and EWPL have improved during the year, ESPL's profitability was impacted by subdued capital market activity and muted inflow, amid challenging macroeconomic headwinds. Resultantly, ECL’s revenue rose to Rs 181 crore in fiscal 2026, from Rs 169 crore a year back, and EWPL’s revenue rose to Rs 120 crore from Rs 87 crore. ESPL’s revenue declined marginally to Rs 155 crore in fiscal 2026 from Rs 161 crore in fiscal 2025.             

 

Further, consolidated profitability was also affected by one-time expenses associated with setting up new business verticals, including AIFs, custody services, the NBFC platform, acquisition-related costs, and technology investments.

 

Notwithstanding moderation in profitability, the group's earnings mix has been diversifying over the years in line with its expanding presence across multiple non-broking businesses. Based on the fiscal 2026 revenue mix, ESPL, ECL and EWPL contributed 38%, 44% and 29% of the consolidated revenue, respectively. This represents a structural reduction in the group's dependence on the more volatile broking income, which accounted for 44% of total revenue in fiscal 2020.

 

For the first quarter of fiscal 2027, the group reported PAT of Rs 10 crore. In the near to medium term, the share of revenue from wealth management business is expected to increase, aided by acquisition of Sapient, Symphonia and Credence, whereas broking income and income from investment banking business is expected to comprise the balance 55%-60% in equal halves.

 

Apart from cross-selling opportunities, this diversity partly insulates the income profile from volatility across market cycles. Translation of higher revenue from the wealth management business into ultimate profitability will remain key as the business scales up.

 

Inherent exposure to competitive intensity and uncertainties in the capital-market-related businesses, including regulatory changes

With capital market-linked businesses forming the major product suite, the group remains susceptible to vagaries of the market. As corporate and investor sentiments drive investment banking related activities and portfolio flows in the wealth management space, business and earnings remain exposed to cyclicality and volatility in capital markets and various other political, social and macroeconomic factors.

 

The group is also exposed to regulatory risk. Unlike lending operations, wealth management is largely fee-based, and thus, any credit event has a relatively lower impact on the capital base. However, these businesses operate in a highly regulated environment, and any unanticipated change in regulations could adversely impact the business model. For instance, in the last few years, regulations that prohibited upfront commissions, led to a sharp erosion in commission income. Many players saw their margin getting eroded as they have adapted, or are in the process of modifying, their business models. Similarly, in the broking business, regulation on the upfront margin requirement by the Securities Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has increased the borrowing requirements of players, thereby impacting their leverage and earnings.

 

While group has built a comprehensive platform to cater to multiple client segments through a wide range of products - which partially mitigates the risk, any regulatory change that could adversely impact the business, will remain key monitorable.

Liquidity Adequate

As on August 31, 2026, the group had consolidated unencumbered liquidity of Rs 311 crore against upcoming repayment of Rs 211 crore over the succeeding six months.

Outlook Stable

Crisil Ratings believes that the Equirus group benefits from presence of an experienced leadership team and high-pedigree investors, and from its established track record in the institutional broking segment. Its ability to profitably scale non-broking businesses and maintain diversity in income profile, remain key.

Rating sensitivity factors

Upward factors

  • Significant and sustained improvement in market position across businesses
  • Diversification in earnings profile, enhancing stability in profitability, marked by consolidated cost to income improving to, and remaining below, 60% on a sustained basis.

 

Downward factors

  • Sustained deterioration in earnings and/or capitalisation metrics, with gearing exceeding 3 times for a prolonged period
  • Impact on business risk profile, indicated by drop in market share impacting revenues from core operations
  • Regulatory actions in any business segment of the group, thereby weakening the overall business risk profile

About the Company

About the Group

The Equirus group has been promoted by Ajay Rameshchandra Garg, who holds 41.46% stake in the holding entity – ECL directly and indirectly, through Sanron Management Services LLP. Incepted in 2007, the group commenced operations as an institutional broking service provider in 2010. Presently, the group is engaged in equity capital markets, portfolio management services, wealth management, fixed income, and insurance broking. ECPL, the group's holding company, which offers investment banking services, was incorporated in 2011. EWPL, on the other hand, is the wealth and portfolio management arm.
